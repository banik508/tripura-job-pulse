You will be responsible for developing and executing comprehensive business strategies that position our organization for sustainable expansion while maintaining operational excellence and profitability. • Develop and implement strategic business plans for emerging territories, identifying market opportunities and establishing competitive positioning • Lead, mentor, and develop management, and customer relationship development • Conduct comprehensive market analysis and competitive intelligence to inform decision-making and identify emerging trends • Manage P&L responsibilities, including budget planning, resource allocation, and financial performance monitoring • Build and maintain strong relationships with key stakeholders, clients, and cross-functional teams across the organization • Establish operational processes and systems that ensure scalability and efficiency as the territory expands • Monitor performance metrics and KPIs, utilizing data-driven insights to optimize business outcomes and inform strategic adjustments • Represent the organization in regional business forums and industry events to enhance brand visibility and market presence • Collaborate with corporate leadership to align territorial strategies with organizational objectives and long-term vision Qualifications • Minimum 8 years of senior management experience,
