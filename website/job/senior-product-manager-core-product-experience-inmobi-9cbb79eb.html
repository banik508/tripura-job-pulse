To learn more, visit glance.com. About Accelerate: Accelerate is InMobi’s AI-native, self-serve campaign management platform for SMBs, D2C brands, agencies and enterprise advertisers. An advertiser describes their brand and goals; Accelerate’s agents plan, create, launch and optimize campaigns across ad platforms such as TikTok and OpenAI, with more supply integrations on the way. We are building toward a fully autonomous marketing platform. This role sits on the demand side, reporting to the Group Product Manager for Customer Experience Core. About the role: You will own the heart of Accelerate: the experience that turns a brand’s goals into a live, running campaign. That includes campaign creation, the chat interface advertisers use to work with our agents, and AI creative generation. You will make that one experience work for very different customers: an SMB owner launching a first campaign, an agency running dozens of client accounts, a mid-size advertiser scaling spend, and an enterprise team with approval processes. You will also take Accelerate beyond our own app, through APIs for companies that want our capabilities without onboarding, and through bundled offerings inside SMB-heavy platforms such as Stripe, Razorpay and Shopify. This is a senior, high-ownership role. The product is agentic and LLM-driven, so you will shape not just screens but how the agent reasons, what it produces, and how we measure whether it is good. What you will own: • Campaign creation, chat and creative generation. The hero surface where an advertiser’s brand blueprint becomes a live campaign. You own the end-to-end flow, the conversation design, and the quality of what the agent produces. • Segment-specific journeys. One product that adapts to SMBs, agencies, mid-size advertisers and enterprise clients. Includes multi-account workflows for agencies and controls, approvals and roles for enterprise. • AI quality for your surfaces. Prompt and RAG design for brand context, eval sets that define “good” for a campaign plan or a creative, and quality gates that releases must pass. The Agentic AI pod owns platform-wide autonomy tiers and guardrails; you own whether your surfaces produce the right output. • APIs and headless access.
