About the job
Job Description Role Summary As City Lead for DineOut 2.0, you will own Digital Menu (DM) adoption and transactions across Bangalore and the additional cities assigned to your cluster. You will lead a team of Partner Success Managers and Service Executives who take restaurants from sign-up to full deployment, staff adoption and steady order and payment volumes through Swiggy. This is a player-coach role. You carry team goals for the cluster and individual goals of your own, including personally landing and activating the anchor brands that set the pace in each city. DineOut 2.0 is scaling fast, so the job is as much about building the playbook (what works in Bangalore and how to repeat it in the next city) as it is about running it. You will work closely with Sales, Content, Product, Engineering and PoS partners, and you will be equally at home in a restaurant owner’s office, on the floor with a captain, and in a business review with leadership. Roles & Responsibilities ● Own the cluster outcome: Be accountable for Digital Menu orders per day, conversion to bill payment through Swiggy, and cost per order across Bangalore and the other cities in your cluster. ● Lead and grow the team: Manage, coach and develop Partner Success Managers and Service Executives. Allocate territories and micro-markets, hire and onboard, and run a weekly performance rhythm against clear targets. ● Drive the restaurant lifecycle end to end: Ensure every restaurant moves smoothly from sign- up to PoS integration, menu digitisation, deployment, staff training and steady usage, and bring down the time from sign-off to full go-live. ● Win the floor, not just the owner: Design and run activation and incentive programms for managers, captains and service staff so the digital menu becomes the default way to order and pay, not an add-on to the physical menu. ● Convert traffic into payments: Work with Product and restaurants to close the leakage between scan, order and bill payment, including pay-bill enablement, on-ground reconciliation and payment-offer hooks. ● Land and grow anchor brands: Build and manage relationships with anchor brands, chains and multi-outlet groups. Secure the top brands in each city first and use them as references to create pull across the market. ● Manage PoS and cross-team dependencies: Coordinate with PoS partners and internal Content, Tech and Product teams to unblock integrations, menu changes and edge cases quickly. Escalate and track every blocker to closure. ● Build and scale the city playbook: Identify which restaurant types and micro-markets adopt fastest, document what works, and take the playbook live in new cities. Plan local campaigns with Marketing and represent Swiggy at restaurant associations and local F&B events. ● Run the business on data and the voice of the restaurant: Maintain lead lists, deployment funnels and adoption and conversion dashboards to spot struggling restaurants early, and communicate product and process needs from the field to HQ. Goals & Success Measures This role carries both team goals and individual (IC) goals. Specific targets and the weightage between the two are set quarterly. Team goals (shared with your Partner Success Managers and Service Executives) ● Restaurants live on Digital Menu in the cluster, with a focus on high-traffic, high-potential outlets. ● Deployment turnaround time from sign-off to full go-live. ● Adoption depth: share of live restaurants with healthy weekly scans and ordering. ● Conversion: bill payments through Swiggy as a share of orders placed, and pay-bill enablement coverage. ● Orders per day and incremental share of wallet at live restaurants. ● Team health: attrition, ramp-up time for new joiners and restaurants managed per team member. Individual (IC) goals ● Personally close and activate a defined set of anchor brands and multi-outlet groups every quarter.…
