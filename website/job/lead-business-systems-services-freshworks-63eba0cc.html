About the job
Job Description This role serves as the Solution Architect and strategic technical owner for the Customer Support technology ecosystem. The architect will partner closely with Customer Support Operations, Customer Experience (CX), Product, Engineering, and IT teams to design scalable, secure, and high-performing solutions across Freshdesk , customer support platforms, and enterprise integrations. The ideal candidate brings deep experience with one or more leading customer support platforms such as Freshdesk, Zendesk, Salesforce Service Cloud, ServiceNow Customer Service Management (CSM), or similar enterprise service management solutions , along with strong expertise in integrations, automation, AI, and enterprise architecture. As a Manager-equivalent Individual Contributor , this role provides architectural leadership while remaining hands-on in solution design, platform configuration, integrations, automation, and complex technical problem-solving. Key Responsibilities • Own the architecture and technical roadmap for Customer Support applications and related integrations. • Design scalable solutions across Freshdesk , knowledge management, chat, messaging, telephony, CRM, and enterprise applications. • Provide architectural guidance for platform configuration, automation, integrations, and customizations. • Design and govern integrations using REST APIs, webhooks, middleware, and event-driven architectures. • Establish best practices for application architecture, security, data governance, and platform scalability. • Lead architecture reviews for major implementations, platform upgrades, migrations, and transformation initiatives. • Drive platform standardization while reducing technical debt and improving operational efficiency. • Evaluate and implement AI capabilities including ticket summarization, intelligent routing, virtual agents, knowledge recommendations, and agent productivity enhancements. • Partner with business stakeholders to identify opportunities for automation and continuous improvement. • Mentor administrators, business systems analysts, and engineers on architecture standards and best practices. Key Decisions Responsible For • Customer Support application architecture and technology roadmap. • Case management and workflow architecture. • Omnichannel support design including email, chat, messaging, and voice. • Integration architecture and middleware strategy. • Automation and AI strategy. • Platform governance and security. • Build vs. configure vs. integrate decisions. • Long-term scalability and platform modernization. Technical Skills • Strong experience architecting enterprise Customer Support platforms such as Freshdesk, Zendesk, Salesforce Service Cloud, ServiceNow CSM , or similar solutions. • Deep understanding of customer support operations including case management, ticketing, SLAs, routing, queues, knowledge management, and omnichannel support. • Experience with REST APIs, middleware platforms, webhooks, and enterprise integrations. • Experience implementing workflow automation using platform-native capabilities and low-code/no-code tools. • Knowledge of CRM integrations and customer lifecycle processes. • Experience with AI-powered customer service capabilities and Generative AI technologies. • Strong understanding of enterprise application architecture, security, data modelling, and governance. • Ability to troubleshoot complex application and integration issues across multiple platforms. Leadership Skills • Ability to influence architecture decisions across business and technology teams. • Strong stakeholder management and communication skills. • Proven experience leading enterprise platform implementations and transformation initiatives. • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
