Key responsibilities: • Online fraud detection such as collusion, multiple ids, bonus abuse etc. using data analytics. • Use analytics techniques to detect, prevent and mitigate fraud. Fraud hypothesis and identifying new patterns of abuse • Work on Insights and help in translating data into actionable business decisions. • Solutioning with Business & Product team by bringing in the right process/ system/ policy fixes in a manner that balances b/w cost & experience • Defining key Financial and Operational metric to enable real time detection of fraud/ abuse • Own daily, weekly and monthly reporting of KPIs. • Build strategies and suggest changes in processes which will eventually become industry benchmarks in the risk management space. • Analyzing behavior of new users and old users for identifying new patterns which may help in early detection of risk. • Explore new features, external engagements and features/variables for better Risk mitigation.
