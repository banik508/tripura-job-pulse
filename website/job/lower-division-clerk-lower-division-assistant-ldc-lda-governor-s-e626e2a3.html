How to apply
Eligible and willing LDC/LDA employees currently serving in Government of Tripura departments must apply through the proper channel. Complete the prescribed deputation application form, attach attested copies of the latest ACR/APR, and submit through the Head of the Parent Department by 10 November 2026. The Head of Department must forward the application with service-record verification and confirmation regarding pending or contemplated vigilance/disciplinary cases.
About the job
Group C (Non-Gazetted), deputation basis. Applicants must already be serving as an LDC/LDA under the Government of Tripura. Computer-operation proficiency and minimum accurate English typing speed of 30 words per minute are required. Attested copies of the latest ACR/APR must be submitted. Initial deputation period: 3 years, subject to extension based on administrative requirements. Pay and allowances as per Government of Tripura guidelines. Selection arrangements, if applicable, will be communicated.
