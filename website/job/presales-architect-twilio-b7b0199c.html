About the job This position is needed to drive technical wins and design scalable customer engagement solutions using Twilio's API platform. As a Presales Architect, you will act as the trusted technical advisor for our most strategic enterprise prospects. You will bridge the gap between complex business problems and Twilio's powerful communication APIs, crafting innovative architectures that drive customer success. By partnering closely with the sales team, you will lead technical discovery, build compelling proofs-of-concept, and demonstrate how Twilio can transform a company's customer engagement strategy. Responsibilities In this role, you’ll: • Partner with enterprise sales executives to lead technical discovery sessions, understanding complex business requirements and translating them into comprehensive Twilio solutions. • Design and architect scalable, secure, and resilient communication workflows using Twilio's suite of APIs (SMS, Voice, Video, Flex, etc.) tailored to client-specific use cases. • Develop and deliver compelling technical demonstrations, custom prototypes, and proofs-of-concept (POCs) that clearly articulate the business value of the Twilio platform. • Mitigate technical risks and overcome objections during the sales cycle by providing authoritative guidance on security, compliance, integration patterns, and architectural best practices. • Collaborate with product and engineering teams to relay critical market feedback, feature requests, and emerging trends from the field to influence the Twilio roadmap. Qualifications Twilio values diverse experiences from all kinds of industries, and we encourage everyone who meets the required qualifications to apply. If your career is just starting or hasn't followed a traditional path, don't let that stop you from considering Twilio.
