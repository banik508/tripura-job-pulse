About the job
Inizio Ignite, Putnam Inizio Ignite is the global advisory partner for health and life sciences, encompassing connected expertise across Research Partnership, Putnam, Vynamic, and STEM. Guided by purpose, passion, and precision, we collaborate with clients to ignite impact, accelerate performance, and deliver lasting change for patients. By uniting strategy, insights, and innovation with a unique depth and breadth of expertise, we drive transformation and shape the future of health.  Our Putnam strategy consulting team bridges science and strategy to enable confident decision-making and build value for pharma. We partner with leading health and life science companies serving brand & commercial leaders, Medical Affairs, access & pricing, and R&D.  Our expertise spans offerings including therapeutic area strategy, data & AI strategy, go-to-market strategy, commercialization & growth, value, pricing & access (VPA) strategy, and evidence generation. Proudly part of Inizio – the market-leading commercialization partner for health and life sciences. From early-stage development to loss of exclusivity, we simplify complexity, unlock value, and make collaboration seamless at every pivotal moment – supporting confident, insight-driven decisions that move innovation forward.  Role Overview A dynamic and proactive professional with experience in Systematic Literature Reviews (SLR) and healthcare research. Skill and Expertise - FOR SLR BACKGROUND What you'll be doing • Support the design and development of systematic review protocols. • Conduct literature searches across healthcare databases. • Perform structured and targeted literature reviews to support evidence generation activities. • Screen abstracts and full-text articles according to study criteria. • Extract and organize relevant clinical, epidemiological, economic, and humanistic outcomes data. • Conduct quality assessment of identified studies. • Support data synthesis and reporting activities. • Assist in preparing technical reports, presentations, and manuscripts. • Participate in internal and client discussions as required. What you'll need • Basic understanding of systematic review methodologies and evidence synthesis. • Experience with literature databases such as PubMed, Embase, and Cochrane Library. • Familiarity with EndNote and/or Reference Manager. • Knowledge of data extraction and quality assessment methodologies. • Proficiency in Microsoft Word, Excel, and PowerPoint. • Strong written and verbal communication skills. Educational Qualification - B.Pharm / M.Pharm / PhD Typical Experience - • B Pharm/MPharm candidates: 3-5 years in literature reviews especially SLRs focused at HTA submissions • PhD candidates: 0-2 years if enough SLR experience during academic years • Desirable: Authored manuscripts, posters, publications, or conference presentations related to literature reviews or healthcare research.
