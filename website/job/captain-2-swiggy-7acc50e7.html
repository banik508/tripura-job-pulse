About the job
Job Description About the Role We are looking for a Captain to join Trip Assist, CREW’s on-trip concierge. You’ll be the person our travelers are actually talking to while they’re on their trip. When they want to know tomorrow’s plan, need a dinner spot, or something goes wrong at the hotel, you’re the one who answers, quickly, warmly and correctly. You’ll be assigned parts of live trips. You’ll build and maintain the trip plans, keep travelers informed through the trip, coordinate with hotels, DMCs, guides and vendors, and close every trip properly once it ends. You’ll report to the Assistant Manager, Trip Assist, and work alongside Senior Captains who lead trips end to end. This is a hands-on, execution-heavy role. You’re measured on how fast and accurately you respond, how smoothly the trip runs, and how the traveler rates the experience at the end. We’re looking for someone who has been on the ground (or on the phone) while a trip was actually happening, knows what breaks, and stays calm and quick when it does. Key Responsibilities ● Build the trip plan: Put together an accurate day-by-day itinerary using our service design. Confirm hotel bookings and research sightseeing, dining and activity options, so every day of the trip is planned and timed.● Add thoughtful touches: Go beyond the template where it makes sense, like a local food recommendation, a re-timed slot based on a group preference, or a fun activity that isn’t on the standard list. ● Respond fast and well: Answer traveler queries within minutes, in simple, warm, easy-to-read language. No walls of text. ● Make information visual: Turn plans into checklists, simple maps and neatly formatted day plans so travelers can take them in at a glance. ● Flag escalations immediately: When something goes wrong (a room issue, a delayed flight, a no-show vendor), raise it to the right person right away with full context: what happened, photos, and the options you’ve already checked. ● Triage and suggest next steps: Judge how serious an issue is and come to your TL with a possible solution, not just the problem. ● Coordinate with partners: Work with hotels, DMCs, guides, transport partners and on-ground vendors so the trip runs the way it was promised. ● Follow every process, every time: Run the full trip process without missing a step, including the end-of-trip NPS message, follow-ups, closing and exporting the chat, and paying the guide. Don’t just mark a step as done; make sure it’s actually completed. ● Own the small stuff: Catch timing clashes or gaps in the itinerary and fix them yourself, without waiting to be told. ● Use AI tools in your day-to-day workflow, and flag repetitive work that’s worth automating. ● Be available for rotational shifts, including nights, weekends and peak season, so travelers always have someone on the other end. Qualifications Required Skills & Qualifications ● Open to rotational shifts (5-day work week including weekends). ● 1-5 years of relevant experience in at least one of the following: ○ A concierge role (hotel, lifestyle or travel concierge) ○ Handling trips for travelers (trip management, tour coordination, travel desk) ○ On-ground travel operations (DMC, tour operator, ground handling, events or group travel) ● Hands-on vendor coordination: you’ve held a hotel, driver or guide to a service standard while the traveler was waiting. ● Fast, clear and warm written communication, especially over chat. ● Strong ops mindset, bias for action and high ownership. ● Attention to detail in itineraries: timings, bookings and logistics that add up. ● Genuine passion for travel and destination knowledge, with the taste to make a trip feel special.
