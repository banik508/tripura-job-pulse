About the job
Job Description Role Overview The Partner Success Manager / Senior Account Manager will own the post-sales relationship with restaurant partners, ensuring successful adoption, active usage and measurable ROI. The role is central to driving partner satisfaction, retention and revenue growth while acting as a trusted growth advisor to restaurant partners. 1. Partner Onboarding & Adoption • Own end-to-end post-sales onboarding of the product suite for assigned restaurant partners, including Digital Menu, CRM tools, reservations and QR-based ordering. • Coordinate product setup, POS integration, QR deployment and account configuration, including menus, floor plans, shifts and outlet details. • Conduct structured product walkthroughs, demos and staff training to ensure smooth go-live within defined timelines. 2. Partner Success & Relationship Management • Act as the primary point of contact for restaurant partners in the city. • Build strong, long-term relationships with owners, decision-makers and outlet managers. • Regularly engage partners through check-ins, reviews and on-ground visits. 3. Driving ROI & Business Growth • Help restaurant partners translate product feature usage into measurable business outcomes. • Drive higher AOV/table value through upsell and cross-sell opportunities. • Improve repeat visits using CRM and targeted campaigns. • Enable better table utilization and demand shaping. • Reduce operational friction through QR-led ordering and billing. • Track and communicate ROI metrics such as adoption depth, repeat-rate uplift, campaign performance and menu-led revenue impact. 4. Performance Monitoring & Reviews • Conduct monthly and quarterly business reviews (MBRs/QBRs) with partners. • Analyze partner performance data and recommend actionable improvements. • Identify early warning signs of churn and proactively resolve issues. 5. Expansion & Feature Adoption Support • Identify opportunities to increase depth of usage across available product features. • Train restaurant staff on new workflows and maintain adoption through repeat training and SOPs, especially during staff changes. • Support expansion across multiple outlets by demonstrating value and sharing best practices. • Work closely with Sales and City teams to enable renewals and portfolio expansion through strong post-sales outcomes. 6. Product Support & Issue Resolution • Act as the first-level support point for product-related queries and issues. • Coordinate with Product, Tech, Ops and Support teams to resolve bugs, integrations and workflow challenges. • Ensure minimal downtime and a smooth partner experience. 7. Partner Feedback & Continuous Improvement • Act as the voice of restaurant partners by sharing structured feedback on product usability, gaps and enhancement needs. • Contribute inputs to improve product features, onboarding journeys and partner-facing processes. Key Success Metrics (KPIs) • Product feature adoption and activation rate • Partner retention and churn reduction • Usage frequency across product features • Partner ROI and satisfaction metrics, including NPS • Revenue expansion from existing partners Qualifications Experience & Qualifications • 4–7 years of experience in Account Management, Customer Success, Partner Success or B2B relationship management. • Prior experience in F&B;, hospitality, SaaS, CRM, POS or restaurant technology is highly preferred. • Experience managing city-level or portfolio-based accounts is a strong plus. Skills & Competencies • Strong stakeholder management and relationship-building skills. • Good understanding of business metrics, ROI and growth levers. • Comfortable working with data, dashboards and performance reports. • Ability to train and influence non-technical restaurant owners and staff. • High ownership mindset with strong problem-solving abilities. • Excellent communication skills; local language proficiency and English preferred.…
