Editing a plan changes what new buyers get. It never changes what somebody already paid for.

What each payment granted is recorded when you approve it. If you drop Basic from 20 views to 10, everyone who already bought Basic keeps their 20.

Free allowance

Put 999999 here to make every job free for everyone. Anything from 1000 upwards counts as no limit: the site then stops showing a views-left counter and says the jobs are free, rather than printing the number.

Never create a plan priced ₹0. A free plan still sends the person into the payment screen and then waits for you to approve it. If something should be free, raise the allowance above instead.
