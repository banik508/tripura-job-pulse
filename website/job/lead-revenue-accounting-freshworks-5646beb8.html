About the job
Job Description We are seeking a highly analytical and automation driven Revenue Accounting Lead to drive excellence within our revenue accounting organization.. In this high-impact role, you will take ownership of various revenue streams—while acting as a key cross-functional partner to scale our financial operations. If you have deep expertise in ASC 606, a strong background in SOX compliance, and a passion for systems automation and process transformation in a fast-paced environment, this role offers a unique platform to shape our financial infrastructure. Responsibilities • Lead the accounting, analysis, and reporting of revenue streams, such as on-prem revenue, subscription revenue, marketplace revenue, and resellers. • Act as a primary cross-functional liaison with Billings, IT BizApps, and Analytics to identify operational bottlenecks, design solutions, and implement process improvements across all revenue reporting areas. • Drive continuous process improvements and lifecycle transformations to enhance scalability across all revenue reporting areas. • Support holistic business unit reporting and provide high-level financial insights to management. • Master the architecture of financial systems critical to revenue accounting, leading report testing and upstream system changes. • Prepare analysis for ad hoc requests with limited information. • Ability to support external audit related queries. • Collaborate with Internal Audit to establish and maintain a robust SOX/ICFR control environment. Qualifications • Chartered Accountant (CA),required. • 6+ years of relevant experience in revenue accounting, financial operations or audit, with a focus on core operations and automation within SOX/ICFR environment. • Expert-level understanding of ASC 606 and transferable skills across various complex revenue models and processes. • Strong analytical mindset with the ability to translate complex data and process to actionable improvements and insights. • Experience with process transformation projects • High degree of adaptability and the ability to pivot across different areas based on team and business needs. • Proficient in Excel and other Microsoft Office products, with the ability to manage large manual excel models and analyze large amounts of data. • Strong communication skills required, both written and verbal. • Excellent organization skills and ability to manage multiple changing priorities along with day-to-day responsibilities. • Experience with Netsuite, ChargeBee, and Salesforce is a plus • Ability to commute to the Bangalore office three times a week.
