Senior Manager – Company Secretarial & Compliance Location: Bengaluru Function: Finance & Accounts – Corporate Secretarial & Compliance Reports to: Company Secretary & Compliance Officer Role purpose Own key company secretarial and compliance workstreams for Swiggy and its group entities. The role will support Board and shareholder governance, listed-company compliance, insider-trading controls, corporate actions and strategic transactions in a fast-growing, listed-company environment. Key responsibilities • Manage end-to-end Board and Committee meetings, including agendas, notices, papers, resolutions, minutes and action trackers. • Coordinate AGMs, EGMs and postal ballots, including notices, e-voting, scrutiniser, RTA, depository and stock-exchange requirements. • Ensure timely and accurate compliance under the Companies Act, Secretarial Standards, SEBI LODR, SEBI PIT and other applicable laws. • Manage statutory filings, event-based disclosures, registers, minutes and records for Swiggy and relevant subsidiaries. • Operate and strengthen the Prevention of Insider Trading framework, including designated-person governance, trading windows, pre-clearance, disclosures, UPSI records and employee awareness. • Provide secretarial and governance support for M&A, restructuring, ESOPs and other corporate actions. • Drive subsidiary compliance reviews, audit readiness and closure of identified gaps. • Partner with Finance, Legal, HR, Investor Relations, IT, auditors, exchanges, depositories, RTA and external counsel. • Improve processes through standardisation, automation, digitisation and responsible use of AI-enabled tools. • Mentor team members and help build a scalable, high-ownership governance function. Qualifications and experience • Qualified Company Secretary and Associate/Fellow Member of ICSI. • 8+ years of post-qualification experience, preferably with a listed company or a professional-services firm advising listed companies. • Strong knowledge of the Companies Act, SEBI LODR, SEBI PIT, Secretarial Standards and stock-exchange compliances. • Practical experience in Board governance, shareholder meetings, statutory filings, insider trading, corporate actions and subsidiary compliance. • Exposure to capital markets, fundraising, M&A, restructuring, ESOPs, FEMA/FDI or compliance technology is preferred. • Strong drafting, analytical, research and stakeholder-management skills. • High integrity, discretion, ownership and ability to manage multiple time-sensitive priorities.
