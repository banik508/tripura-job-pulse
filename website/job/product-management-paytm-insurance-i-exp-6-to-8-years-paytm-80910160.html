About the job
About Paytm Paytm is India's leading mobile payments and financial services distribution company. Pioneer of the mobile QR payments revolution in India, Paytm builds technologies that help small businesses with payments and commerce. Paytm’s mission is to serve half a billion Indians and bring them to the mainstream economy with the help of technology. About the team: Headquartered in Noida, India, Paytm Insurance Broking Private Limited (PIBPL), a wholly owned subsidiary of One97 Communications (OCL) is an online insurance market place, that offers insurance products across all leading insurance companies, with products across auto, life and health insurance and provide policy management and claim services for our customers. About the role We're looking for product managers who think deeply about consumers and move fast. You'll own a meaningful area of product used by a large and diverse set of Indian consumers, making decisions that show up in people's hands within weeks. This is a role for builders who treat ambiguity as opportunity. What you'll do You'll own the strategy and execution for your product area end to end — from identifying the right problem to shipping and iterating. You'll spend real time understanding users (not just reading research decks), translate insight into crisp product bets, and partner closely with design, engineering, and business to ship. You'll be measured on outcomes, not output, and you'll be expected to drive your area with minimal hand-holding. What we're looking for ● 6–8 years of experience, ideally in entrepreneurship, building consumer products or top-tier consulting. ● Strong consumer intuition — you have opinions about why products work, and you can defend them with both logic and evidence. ● High agency. You define the problem, find the path, and get it done without waiting for permission. ● Sharp analytical and structured thinking, balanced with a bias for action. ● Comfort operating in a fast-paced, high-ownership environment where speed and judgment both matter. Why this matters You’ll be at the cutting edge of tech and finance. You'll build for one of the largest and most diverse consumer bases in the world, ship at scale and at pace. The problems are hard, the impact is immediate, and the autonomy is real. Why join us? ● A collaborative output driven program that brings cohesiveness across businesses through technology ● Improve the average revenue per use by increasing the cross-sell opportunities ● A solid 360 feedback from your peer teams on your support of their goals ● Respect, that is earned, not demanded from your peers and manager Compensation: If you are the right fit, we believe in creating wealth for you With enviable 500 mn+ registered users, 17 mn+ merchants and depth of data in our ecosystem, we are in a unique position to democratize credit for deserving consumers & merchants - and we are committed to it. India's largest digital lending story is brewing here. It's your opportunity to be a part of the story!
