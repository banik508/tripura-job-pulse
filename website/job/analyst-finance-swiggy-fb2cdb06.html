About the job
Job Description Mandate 3 – Employees will work full-time from their base location i.e. Bangalore. Role: Analyst Finance - Accounts Payable About The Role: As an Analyst Finance in Accounts Payable, you will be responsible for the accurate and timely processing of vendor invoices and payments, resolving vendor queries, ensuring statutory compliance, and maintaining healthy vendor relationships as part of the end-to-end procure-to-pay cycle. Your Job: Vendor Payments Prepare payment proposals and payment runs as per agreed payment terms and due dates, in coordination with Treasury. Apply maker-checker controls to prevent duplicate, incorrect or unapproved payments. Manage advance payments, bank rejections / failed payments and re-processing, and post payments correctly in the books. Track MSME vendor payments and ensure compliance with statutory payment timelines. Share payment advices and remittance details with vendors. Validate vendor payment files for accuracy of vendor, bank and amount details before release. Perform fund allocation and coordinate payment approvals with Treasury and approvers. Process BG vendor payments as per approvals and agreed terms. Vendor Queries and Support Act as the point of contact for vendor queries on invoice status, payment status, TDS/GST deductions and short payments, and respond within defined TAT. Manage the AP vendor helpdesk / shared mailbox and track queries to closure. Coordinate with internal stakeholders (Procurement, Category, Tax, Treasury) to resolve vendor issues. Provide TDS certificates (Form 16A) and account statements to vendors on request. Maintain vendor master data accuracy (bank details, GSTIN, PAN, MSME status, payment terms) with proper approvals. Lead the weekly vendor call, covering open invoices, payment status, pending issues and action items. Statutory Compliance Ensure correct computation and deduction of TDS and GST (including RCM, where applicable), and support timely filing and reconciliation. Support statutory, internal and external audits with required documentation. Monitoring, Team Coordination and Audit Perform regular system checks on the Vendor Payable Dashboard and Top Vendor Reconciliation Dashboard, and follow up on exceptions and overdue items. Oversee overall team activities and ensure deadlines and quality of AP deliverables are met. Manage audit requirements, including preparation and submission of payment and vendor documentation. Vendor Reconciliation and Month-End Perform periodic vendor ledger reconciliations, request vendor statements, and follow up with vendors and Procurement on pending statements. Analyse and resolve discrepancies, update the books accordingly, and obtain NDC (No Dues Certificate) from vendors where required. Support month-end and quarter-end closing, including accruals and provisions, and review AP ageing and overdue payables. Identify process gaps and support automation and process improvement initiatives. Who is the best fit? 4+ Years of experience in the end-to-end procure-to-pay process, preferably in the Ecommerce industry. Strong understanding of Accounts Payable functions including vendor set-up, invoice processing, vendor payments, statutory deductions (TDS/GST) and vendor management. Good knowledge of vendor reconciliation and month-end closing activities. Strong skills in process controls, MS Office suite (Word, Excel, PowerPoint) and ERP systems (Zoho preferred). Customer-service-driven individual with the ability to multitask, manage deadlines and work in time-sensitive situations. Problem-solver who can identify and resolve issues, with good written and verbal communication skills for dealing with vendors and internal stakeholders. Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, or equivalent. Skills:…
