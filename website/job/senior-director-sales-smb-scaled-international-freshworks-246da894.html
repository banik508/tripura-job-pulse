About the job
Job Description This role would report to: VP and GM, International Sales. Direct reports: the Head of New Business and Expansion (SMB/Digital) and the Senior Manager, EX Account Management (Scaled). As Senior Director of Sales for International SMB and Scaled, you will lead both organizations and the leaders who run them. You should be ready to challenge the status quo, work with the teams to improve the customer experience and journey, and bring the best of each organization together so the whole unit is more successful. This is a role for a senior leader with the experience and total focus to unlock the next stage of growth in both businesses. What you will do • Set the vision, targets and operating rhythm for both teams, with clear ownership of pipeline health, forecast accuracy, sales productivity and CRM discipline. • Challenge how we work today, including handoffs, account transitions, segmentation and coverage, and make evidence based changes that protect retention and NPS. • Improve the customer experience and journey from first contact through new business, expansion and Field, reducing disruptive handoffs and customer fatigue. • Identify what is working well in each team, share it, and standardise where it raises performance across both. • Build a joined up global partner approach, including consistent partner tagging and migration practice. • Work closely with Marketing to drive engagement and create more opportunities for both teams. • Coach and develop the leaders and managers beneath you, and build a strong succession pipeline. • Partner with Sales Development, Pre Sales, Sales Operations, Customer Success, Legal and Finance to deliver one joined up go to market approach. • Apply the discount approval framework consistently, and escalate where required. Qualifications What you bring • Senior leadership of large, multi region sales organisations in software or SaaS, covering both high velocity SMB and more complex, higher value selling. • A proven record of exceeding targets, growing market share and improving customer outcomes, not just new bookings. • Experience bringing new business and account management or expansion teams together, and fixing broken handoffs. • Strong coaching and talent development, with a track record of building leaders and succession. • Operating rigour, including data led decisions, disciplined CRM use and honest forecasting. • Credibility with partners, and a collaborative style with Marketing, Sales Operations, Legal and Finance. • The ability to engage senior customer stakeholders and influence at executive level. • Flexibility to work across time zones and geographies with regional teams and customers. • Confidence to challenge constructively, and the influence to bring people with you.
