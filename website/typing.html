How to get faster

  • Rest your left fingers on A S D F and your right on J K L semicolon. Both thumbs on the space bar.
  • Keep your eyes on the screen, not the keyboard. It feels slower for a week, then much faster.
  • Accuracy first, speed second. Fixing mistakes costs more time than typing carefully.
  • Practise 15 minutes a day rather than two hours once a week.
  • Sit straight, wrists level, screen at eye height. Sore wrists end practice early.

How the test works

Two minutes, one passage, no restarts once the timer begins. To qualify for a certificate you need at least 20 words per minute with 85 per cent accuracy. The test itself is free, and you only pay if you want the certificate.
