About the job
About Zeta At Zeta tech innovation is married with exceptional design. We are crafting the best Digital Banking Experiences for the largest financial institutions across the globe encompassing credit cards, commercial credit, deposits, loans, family banking and other financial products and services. Our Product Design Philosophies Delight through Design At Zeta we believe in meticulously crafting digital experiences with delight as a central focus. This philosophy goes beyond mere functionality; it aims to evoke ecstasy in users. The key is to create an emotional connection between the user and the product, transforming routine interactions into memorable experiences. This involves a deep understanding of the product and the user's needs, desires, and pain points, leveraging color, typography, layout to elicit emotion, pleasure and surprise. Outcome driven UX "Outcome-Driven UX" is a design philosophy that extends UX beyond the traditional goals of usability and aesthetic appeal, focusing on explicitly achieving tangible, measurable results such as improving funnel conversion rates, retention, and revenue generation. Every design decision is guided by how it can positively impact these metrics. The user interface and experience are meticulously crafted to not only be user-friendly but also to strategically influence user behavior in a way that contributes directly to the company's overall success. This entails an in depth understanding of the business objectives; rigorous analysis of user behavior; rapid iterative Experimentation leveraging intuition and experience, and data driven decisions. What You'll Do • Own product experiences end-to-end, from discovery to shipped outcomes. • Turn complex financial experiences into simple, intuitive, and delightful products. • Set a high bar for interaction design, visual craft, and attention to detail. • Leverage AI tools and code to rapidly explore, prototype, build, and iterate. • Work closely with product and engineering to ship high-quality experiences. • Drive measurable product outcomes through experimentation and iteration. • Mentor designers and elevate design quality across teams. What We're Looking For • 5+ years of experience designing and shipping digital products. • Exceptional visual and interaction design skills, with strong product judgment. • Hands-on fluency with AI-assisted design and development tools. • Ability to build functional prototypes, beyond static mockups. • Deep understanding of user behaviour, usability, and business outcomes. • Strong ownership, bias for action, and comfort navigating ambiguity. • Ability to influence product direction and raise the quality bar through craft.
