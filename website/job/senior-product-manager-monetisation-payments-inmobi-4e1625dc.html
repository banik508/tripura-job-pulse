To learn more, visit glance.com. About Accelerate: Accelerate is InMobi’s AI-native, self-serve campaign management platform for SMBs, D2C brands, agencies and enterprise advertisers. An advertiser describes their brand and goals; Accelerate’s agents plan, create, launch and optimize campaigns across ad platforms such as TikTok and OpenAI, with more supply integrations on the way. We are building toward a fully autonomous marketing platform. The product team is organized by capability, not segment. This role sits on the demand side, reporting to the Group Product Manager, alongside Customer Experience Core (campaign creation, onboarding and activation). About the role: You will build Accelerate’s payments and money infrastructure from zero. Every dollar or rupee an advertiser puts into Accelerate flows through what you build: how they fund their account, how spend is drawn down across ad platforms, how they are invoiced, refunded and taxed, and how we settle with suppliers. There is no legacy system to inherit. You will define the wallet, the ledger behind it, and the integration with our third-party payments partner (Vyre), then own the revenue metrics that depend on them. Getting this right is what lets an SMB go from signup to live campaign in minutes, and what lets finance trust every number on the dashboard. You will work with a Senior Product Associate on the pod, engineering, Central Design, and InMobi finance, tax and legal teams. What you will own: • Advertiser wallet. A prepaid balance advertisers top up and Accelerate draws down as campaigns spend. Includes funding methods, auto-recharge, low-balance alerts, credit lines for larger accounts, and the rules for how a balance can and cannot be used. • Ledger and reconciliation. The source of truth for every credit, debit, hold, refund and adjustment. Spend reported by each ad platform must reconcile to the ledger, with clear handling for mismatches. • Payments integration. End-to-end integration with Vyre, our third-party payments partner: collections, payment methods, failures and retries, webhooks, and the fallback plan if a provider is down. • Billing, invoicing and tax. Invoices, receipts, GST, TDS on ad spend, and multi-currency handling as we add international advertisers. • Supplier settlement. How we pay out and reconcile with ad platforms, working with the business team that handles supplier contracts.…
