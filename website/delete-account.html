Delete your account

You can delete your Tripura Job Pulse account at any time, free of charge. You do not need to give a reason, and you do not need to ask us.

What gets deleted

Everything we hold that is tied to your login:

  • Your login and email address
  • Your profile — name, phone number, district, date of birth, education, experience, skills and languages
  • Your mock test attempts and scores
  • Your typing test attempts
  • Your skill certificates, and the record behind them
  • Your payment records and any job plan you bought

This cannot be undone. Any certificate you have earned will stop working on our verification page, so if an employer checks it afterwards it will not be found. Download anything you want to keep before you delete.

Job alert emails are a separate list. If you only want those to stop, use the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any alert — that does not touch your account.
