Tripura TET 2026: every date, and what to do before the form opens

Applications open at 4pm on 1 December and close on the 10th. Ten days, and most of the work should be finished before the first one.

TETteachingapplicationsTripura2026

The Teachers' Recruitment Board has set the dates for T-TET 2026, and the application window is short. Ten days, in the middle of December, for an examination most people have waited a year for.

The useful thing to understand is that almost nothing that goes wrong in those ten days is about the form itself. It is about what you did or did not have ready before it opened.

The dates

The board issued its notification on 30 October 2026, then revised the application schedule on 16 November after technical problems with the portal. These are the dates as they now stand:

What When Applications open 1 December 2026, 4:00 PM Applications close 10 December 2026, 4:00 PM Last date to pay the fee 11 December 2026 Admit card download 11 December (4:00 PM) to 17 December (4:00 PM) Paper I — classes I to V 20 December 2026, a Sunday Paper II — classes VI to VIII 3 January 2027, a Sunday

Two things about that table.

The closing time is 4:00 PM, not midnight. A form "closing on the 10th" closes in the afternoon. People lose a whole day to that assumption every single year.

The fee has its own deadline. Submitting the form on the 10th and paying on the 12th means you have not applied. The fee date is the 11th and it is separate.

The examination dates are the ones published with the schedule and described as tentative. Treat the application dates as firm and the examination dates as likely — and watch the board's own notices, because this schedule has already moved once.

Which paper you sit

Paper I is for teaching classes I to V. Paper II is for classes VI to VIII. If you want to be eligible for both, you sit both — they are on different Sundays, a fortnight apart.

Each paper is 150 multiple-choice questions for 150 marks, one mark each. Paper I has five compulsory subjects. Paper II has three compulsory subjects and then a choice worth 60 marks: Mathematics and Science, or Social Studies. Choose that one deliberately, not on the day.

On eligibility and the pass mark, read the official notification rather than any summary, including this one. The qualification rules differ between the two papers and depend on exactly which diploma or degree you hold. There is a relaxation for SC, ST and differently-abled candidates, but the figures quoted on secondary websites contradict each other, so I am not going to repeat a number here that might cost you an application. The board's notice is the only version that counts.

What to do before 1 December

This is the part that matters, and you have until the end of the month.

Your photograph and signature, in the exact format

The single most common reason a form is rejected or cannot be submitted is a photograph in the wrong size, the wrong dimensions, or the wrong file type. It is also the most avoidable.

Find the specification in the notification — pixel dimensions and the maximum file size in KB — and prepare both files now. Our photo and signature resizer does this in your browser, against the exact limits a portal asks for, and tells you if the result has dropped below the minimum quality.

Do it this week. Do not do it at 3:30 PM on the 10th, on a slow connection, in a cyber café queue.

Your documents, read and checked

Have in front of you, as clean scans:

Madhyamik and Higher Secondary certificates and mark sheets Your graduation degree and mark sheets, if Paper II applies to you Your teaching qualification — D.El.Ed., B.El.Ed. or B.Ed. — with every year's mark sheet Caste or disability certificate, if you are claiming a relaxation Residence proof

Check the spelling of your name on each one. Where they disagree with each other — and they often do, by an initial or a middle name — find out now which version the board wants, not during document verification.

An email address and mobile you actually use

Every notice, every correction window, every admit card instruction goes there. Use one you check daily, and one that will still be yours in a year.

The fee, ready to pay

Know the amount for your category and have the means to pay online. Portals slow to a crawl in the last 48 hours, and a payment that fails at 3:55 PM on the 11th is not a payment.

What to do with December

Between the form closing and Paper I there are nine days. That is not enough to learn a syllabus, but it is enough to do three specific things.

Sit a full timed paper. Not questions here and there — 150 questions, timed, in one sitting, to find out what happens to your accuracy in the last half hour. Our mock tests are free and have no limit on attempts.

Fix the child development section. It is compulsory on both papers, it is the section most candidates treat as common sense, and it is the one where marks are won by people who actually read the material rather than guessing from experience. The study modules cover it.

Top up the state material. If your paper includes Tripura content, the general knowledge bank is built for exactly that, with the reasoning behind each answer rather than just the answer.

Nine days used on those three things is worth more than nine days of reading everything again.

One honest thing about what the certificate is

Passing T-TET makes you eligible to apply for teaching posts. It is not an appointment, and it does not place you in a queue. The Chief Minister said so in the Assembly in March, in plain terms.

That is not a reason to skip it — without the certificate you cannot apply at all. But it is a reason to keep applying for other things while you wait, and to treat this as a door rather than a destination. We publish every Tripura vacancy as it opens, and the career roadmap will lay out what else your qualification already lets you apply for.

The form is open for ten days. Make eight of those days unnecessary by being ready before the first one.
