About the job
Job Description What will you work on? • Strategy & Execution: Lead the end-to-end development of a high-impact content strategy for all cities. You’ll ensure our brand DNA remains intact while the regional teams execute flawlessly on the ground. Maintain a strong command of each city’s food and beverage landscape, including new openings, emerging trends, and industry developments, and use that insight to shape the editorial calendar. • Operational & Creative Standards: Work with the team and oversee day-to-day operations across multiple locations, ensuring high-quality, consistent delivery and maintaining creative standards across the pages. Content spans restaurant reviews, menu launches, chef features, and culinary events, with every output reflecting the brand’s identity. • Performance & Optimization: Keep a pulse on the data. You’ll monitor content performance against KPIs, identifying what’s working and pivoting strategies to ensure we’re always winning in the feed. • Business Acumen & Strategic Partnerships: Identify high-value opportunities to partner with the right F&B brands, restaurant groups, beverage companies, and hospitality businesses that align with our brand identity. Leveraging business savvy to drive growth while maintaining editorial integrity. • Industry Relationships & Network: Build and maintain relationships across the F&B ecosystem, including restaurant groups, chefs, restaurateurs, bar owners, PR agencies, and key industry figures. Leverage this network to secure early access to openings, exclusive collaborations, and original stories. • Audience & Community Building: Develop a deep understanding of the target audience and their dining behaviors. Drive community engagement through strategic digital content, localized events, and initiatives that position the brand as the leading voice of each city’s food scene. • Team Leadership & Mentorship: Hire, lead, and inspire a powerhouse team of creatives across all cities. You are responsible for fostering a collaborative culture and looking over their growth within the organization.
