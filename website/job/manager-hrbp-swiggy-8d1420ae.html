About the job
Job Description - A strong, hands-on People & Culture partner - not only an HR operations or administrative resource. - Someone with at least 4-5 years of experience in a creative agency, media company or fast-paced start-up, who understands the pace, ambiguity and personalities within creative teams. - Experience across all key aspects of HR, including performance management, rewards and recognition, L&D, talent acquisition, employee engagement and culture. - A genuine people’s person who can build trust with a predominantly young workforce, keep a pulse on morale and ensure people feel heard, valued and recognised. - Someone who can create clear career paths, capability-building programmes and regular feedback mechanisms so team members understand how they can grow. - Someone who can address uncertainty early, manage anxiety and maintain engagement. - Someone proactive and solution-oriented who is comfortable working in a fast-paced, evolving environment. - Someone who can coach managers to become better people leaders and build a stronger, consistent culture across cities. In short, we need a proactive HRBP who can help shape the culture and talent ecosystem of Dineout Mag - not someone focused primarily on processes and administration. Qualifications Additionally these are the pointers: • Experience supporting content creators, writers, editors, videographers, and creative production teams. • Background in media, digital marketing, advertising, content production, or a similar industry. • Experience managing fast-paced, creative, and deadline-driven teams. Additional Information Ideal Candidate: A proactive HR professional who can build strong stakeholder relationships, improve employee engagement, support business growth, and effectively manage a diverse creative workforce. • Experience managing a workforce of approximately 100–150+ employees. • Strong stakeholder management and employee engagement skills. • Excellent communication, interpersonal, and conflict-resolution abilities.
