Getting it accepted first time

  • Most Tripura and central portals want the photo as a JPG. This tool always produces a JPG.
  • Keep your face filling most of the frame, looking straight ahead, against a plain background.
  • For the signature, sign on white paper with a black or blue pen and photograph it in daylight, then set the background to white here.
  • If the portal names a size this tool does not list, use Custom and type the numbers from the notification.
  • Check the notification itself rather than trusting a size you were sent on WhatsApp.
