About the job
Job Description • Collaborating with our Enterprise customers across all of LATAM to identify and address their most critical business challenges. • Supporting field sales and channel teams through discovery and product demonstrations for direct and partner-led opportunities. • Gathering requirements effectively to clearly define customer problems. • Building and presenting compelling demonstrations of Freshworks products. • Articulating the value of Freshworks solutions to a variety of audiences. • Embracing lifelong learning and continuously developing your skills. • Build trusted relationships with partner presales across LATAM, serving as a technical resource throughout joint pursuits. • Collaborate with partners on customer workshops, tailored demonstrations, solution design, and technical validation to connect Freshworks capabilities to customer outcomes. • Deliver partner activation through hands-on sessions, demo coaching, and reusable assets that help partners independently position and demonstrate Freshworks solutions. • Work with LATAM channel managers and field account teams to align technical support for joint account plans, partner-led opportunities, and regional events. Qualifications • 6-8 years of Solution Engineering experience. • Experience supporting both direct sales and channel partners is preferred, including partner technical activation and joint customer engagements. • Excellent soft skills and presentation abilities; you will rely on these daily! • Proven capability to work on cross-functional teams to address business and technical challenges. • Strong verbal, written, presentation, and interpersonal communication skills. • Proficiency in both Portuguese and Spanish is preferred, including the ability to conduct discovery sessions and product demonstrations with customers and partners across LATAM. • Demonstrated time management skills in a dynamic sales environment; you will manage multiple deals monthly. • Highly driven individuals with a focus on execution and a strong sense of urgency are encouraged to apply. • A Bachelor’s degree is preferred but not required.
