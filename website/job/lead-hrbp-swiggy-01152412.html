About the job
Job Description We are seeking an experienced and strategic Lead - HRBP to join our organization in Mumbai, India. In this pivotal role, you will serve as a trusted HR business partner, driving organizational effectiveness and employee engagement across multiple business units. You will combine strategic HR expertise with strong business acumen to deliver measurable impact on organizational performance and culture. • Partner strategically with business leaders to understand organizational objectives and translate them into effective HR strategies and initiatives • Lead talent acquisition, development, and retention strategies to build high-performing teams aligned with business goals • Manage employee relations matters, including conflict resolution, performance management, and disciplinary processes, while ensuring compliance with Indian labor laws • Develop and implement organizational development programs to enhance organizational capability, culture, and employee engagement • Oversee performance management processes and provide coaching to managers on effective performance conversations and feedback • Analyze HR metrics and workforce data to provide actionable insights and recommendations for business improvement • Lead cross-functional projects and change management initiatives to support organizational transformation • Mentor and develop HR team members, fostering a culture of continuous learning and professional growth • Ensure HR policy implementation, compliance, and best practices across assigned business units • Collaborate with HR partners to design and execute employee engagement and retention programs Qualifications **Required Qualifications:** • Minimum 8 years of progressive HR experience with at least 3 years in a Human Resources Business Partner capacity • Demonstrated expertise in strategic HR business partnering and organizational development • Strong knowledge of Indian labor laws, employment regulations, and compliance requirements • Proven experience in talent management, employee relations, and performance management • Excellent analytical skills with the ability to interpret HR metrics and workforce data • Strong stakeholder management and relationship-building capabilities • Demonstrated experience in change management and organizational transformation initiatives • Exceptional communication and interpersonal skills with the ability to influence at all organizational levels • Proficiency in managing HRIS systems and HR technology platforms • Strong project management and organizational skills **Preferred Qualifications:** • Experience in coaching and mentoring senior leaders and HR professionals • Background in diversity, equity, and inclusion (DEI) initiatives • Familiarity with compensation and benefits administration • Experience in multiple industry sectors or geographies • Certification in HR management or related professional credentials Additional Information Ideal Candidate: A proactive HR professional who can build strong stakeholder relationships, improve employee engagement, support business growth, and effectively manage a diverse creative workforce. • Experience managing a workforce of approximately 100–150+ employees. • Strong stakeholder management and employee engagement skills. • Excellent communication, interpersonal, and conflict-resolution abilities.
