About the job
Job Description Role Objective The objective of this team is to identify, onboard, and grow premium, high-CFT and trending outlets on Dineout, with a strong focus on being the first mover in acquiring high-potential restaurants before competitors such as District. The team will initially focus on premium outlet acquisition and onboarding, with a long-term mandate to also own account management, GOV growth, market share, and exclusive campaigns/events for these strategic accounts. Key Responsibility Areas (KRAs) 1. Identify Premium & High-Potential Outlets • Identify premium restaurants and high-CFT outlets across priority markets. • Track trending, upcoming, and high-demand restaurants that have strong customer pull. • Build a pipeline of strategically important outlets based on brand value, CFT potential, customer demand, and market relevance. • Proactively identify new restaurant launches and emerging premium brands. 2. Close Comp-Gaps for Premium Outlets • Identify premium outlets that are already live on Z but are missing from Dineout. • Prioritize and close these Comp-Gaps to ensure strong outlet coverage. • Develop a systematic approach to track, prioritize, and convert high-value Z-Gap opportunities. 3. First-Mover Advantage • Ensure Dineout is the first mover in onboarding high-value premium outlets, wherever possible. • Track upcoming launches, competitor movements, and market trends to identify opportunities before other competitors. • Build strong relationships with restaurant owners, management teams, and key decision-makers to secure early partnerships. • Create a culture of proactive acquisition rather than reactive competitor-led onboarding. Long-Term Scope of the Team The role is expected to evolve beyond acquisition into end-to-end ownership of strategic premium accounts. 1. Account Management • Build account management capabilities within the team for outlets onboarded by them. • Maintain strong relationships with premium restaurant partners. • Drive partner engagement, retention, and business growth after onboarding. • Identify opportunities to deepen the relationship and increase business contribution. 2. Drive GOV Growth & Market Share • Own and drive GOV growth from the premium outlet portfolio. • Increase Dineout's market share within premium restaurant segments. • Identify opportunities to improve outlet performance, customer transactions, and overall business contribution. 3. Exclusive Campaigns & Events • Identify premium partners suitable for exclusive campaigns, partnerships, and events. • Work closely with internal teams to conceptualize and execute differentiated campaigns. • Leverage Scenes to create visibility and demand for premium restaurant partners. • Build exclusive restaurant-led experiences and campaigns that strengthen Dineout's proposition for both consumers and restaurant partners. Key Success Metrics The performance of the team can be evaluated through: • Number and quality of premium/high-CFT outlets identified. • Premium outlet onboarding rate. • Reduction in Z-Gaps. • First-mover onboarding rate versus competitors. • GOV generated from newly onboarded premium outlets. • GOV growth from managed accounts. • Dineout market share within the premium outlet segment. • Number and quality of exclusive campaigns/events launched. Qualifications • Strong business development and sales orientation. • Ability to identify and pursue high-value opportunities proactively. • Strong understanding of the premium restaurant/F&B ecosystem. • Excellent relationship-building and negotiation skills. • Ability to work with data and identify business opportunities. • Ability to transition from pure acquisition into strategic account management Additional Information Visit our tech blogs to learn more about some the challenges we deal with: https://medium.com/swiggy-bytes…
