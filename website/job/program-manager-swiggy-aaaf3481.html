About the job
Job Description We're looking for an experienced and detail-oriented Manager - Ops Program Manager to join our operations team in Bengaluru, India. In this role, you will oversee the planning, execution, and optimization of operational programs while leading cross-functional teams to drive efficiency and achieve organizational objectives. The ideal candidate will demonstrate strong leadership capabilities, exceptional organizational skills, and a proven ability to manage complex programs in a fast-paced environment. • Develop, plan, and execute operational programs aligned with organizational strategy and business goals • Oversee program timelines, budgets, and resource allocation to ensure successful delivery within scope and schedule • Identify process inefficiencies and implement improvements to enhance operational performance and reduce costs • Coordinate with multiple stakeholders across departments to ensure alignment, communication, and successful program execution • Monitor key performance indicators (KPIs) and program metrics; prepare regular reports and dashboards for senior management • Manage risks proactively by identifying potential issues, developing mitigation strategies, and implementing contingency plans • Lead, mentor, and develop operations team members; foster a culture of accountability and continuous improvement • Establish and maintain standard operating procedures (SOPs) to ensure consistency and compliance across operations • Collaborate with IT, finance, and other departments to support technology implementations and operational initiatives • Conduct regular program reviews and post-implementation assessments to capture lessons learned and drive future improvements Qualifications • 5+ years of experience in operations management, program management, or related field • 2+ years of experience in a managerial or supervisory capacity • Proven expertise in program planning, execution, and delivery • Strong knowledge of process improvement methodologies and operational best practices • Demonstrated proficiency in budget management and financial analysis • Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to interpret data and make informed decisions • Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel and experience with project management tools • Strong leadership and team management capabilities with the ability to motivate and develop team members • Exceptional communication and interpersonal skills; ability to work effectively with diverse stakeholders • Experience with process optimization and operational efficiency initiatives • Preferred: Agile/Scrum certification or experience with Agile methodologies • Preferred: Six Sigma, Lean, or similar process improvement certification • Preferred: Experience with ERP systems or enterprise software platforms • Preferred: Experience in a matrix organizational structure Additional Information Visit our tech blogs to learn more about some the challenges we deal with: https://medium.com/swiggy-bytes We are an equal opportunity employer and all qualified applicants will receive consideration for employment without regard to race, colour, religion, sex, disability status, or any other characteristic protected by the law.
