About the job
Job Description We are looking for a commercially driven, hands-on leader to own growth and retention across NA region. This role combines Account Management and Customer Success, with accountability for accelerating expansion, controlling churn, and achieving or exceeding net revenue targets. As a player-coach, you will lead critical customer engagements, build predictable execution, strengthen AM/CSM capabilities, and establish a culture of ownership, accountability and collaboration. Key Responsibilities Drive expansion and net revenue Own expansion and net revenue outcomes; accelerate upsell, cross-sell and whitespace opportunities through strong account planning, pipeline creation and deal execution. Identify gaps early and drive recovery plans to achieve or exceed targets. Protect retention and prevent churn Own renewals, gross retention and churn prevention. Proactively identify at-risk customers, establish clear mitigation plans, and personally lead critical renewal, save and escalation conversations. Strengthen adoption, customer value and executive engagement. Build a predictable, data-driven business Own forecasts for expansion, renewals, churn and net revenue. Establish daily execution and weekly pipeline/risk reviews, supported by clear input metrics such as customer engagement, pipeline progression, adoption and renewal readiness. Improve forecast accuracy through evidence-based reviews and timely interventions. Lead and coach a high-performing team Coach Account Managers and Customer Success Managers on commercial execution and customer success fundamentals. Set clear goals and productivity standards, join customer calls, model best practices, develop talent and address underperformance directly and fairly. Raise operational discipline and team culture Build a culture of accountability, urgency, professionalism and collaboration. Set expectations for punctuality, preparation, customer engagement and follow-through. Address behaviors that undermine morale or performance, keeping discussions constructive, fact-based and focused on outcomes. Lead from the front and collaborate Demonstrate strong consultative selling, account planning, negotiation, adoption, value realization and renewal skills. Stay close to portfolio execution and partner with Sales, Support, Product, Finance and other teams to resolve issues and unlock growth. Qualifications • 10+ years of overall experience, preferably in B2B SaaS, Account Management, Customer Success, renewals or expansion. • A strong track record of delivering expansion, retention and net revenue results, with solid AM and CSM fundamentals. • Commercial acumen, consultative selling, customer relationship management and complex negotiation skills. • Strong analytical and forecasting capabilities, with experience using leading indicators and performance metrics to drive action. • Ability to coach, manage performance, build team capability and foster an accountable, collaborative culture. • A self-driven, hands-on mindset with urgency, ownership and the ability to balance leadership with direct customer execution. People management experience is an added advantage; strong individual contributors with demonstrated coaching and leadership capability will also be considered. Measures of Success Achieve or exceed expansion and net revenue targets; improve retention and reduce preventable churn; deliver healthy pipeline and reliable forecasts; raise customer engagement and team productivity; strengthen AM/CSM capability; and build a high-performance culture grounded in ownership and accountability.
