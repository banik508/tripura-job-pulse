About the job
Job Description We are looking for a highly motivated, results-driven Senior Account Executive to accelerate Freshworks' growth across Southern Brazil, covering Paraná, Santa Catarina, and Rio Grande do Sul. The ideal candidate will bring deep regional market knowledge, an established professional network, and a proven track record of acquiring new customers and expanding strategic accounts within the SaaS and enterprise technology ecosystem. This role requires a strong Hunter mindset, combined with the relationship-building and account-development capabilities of a successful Farmer. The successful candidate will be responsible for building a sustainable business across the territory, developing executive relationships, generating new business opportunities, and maximizing revenue growth within existing accounts. Key Responsibilities New Business Development – Hunter • Drive new logo acquisition across Paraná, Santa Catarina, and Rio Grande do Sul. • Proactively identify, prospect, and develop opportunities within Mid-Market and Enterprise accounts. • Leverage an established regional network to accelerate engagement with strategic prospects and key decision-makers. • Build and maintain strong relationships with CIOs, CTOs, IT Directors, Customer Experience leaders, and other C-level executives. • Own the entire sales cycle, from prospecting and qualification through negotiation and closing. • Develop and execute territory and account plans to consistently achieve revenue and new business targets. Account Management & Expansion – Farmer • Build long-term strategic relationships with existing Freshworks customers across the assigned territory. • Identify and execute upselling, cross-selling, and expansion opportunities. • Expand Freshworks' footprint through additional products, business units, and use cases. • Collaborate with Customer Success and other internal teams to maximize customer value, adoption, retention, and growth. • Develop executive sponsorship and multilevel relationships within strategic accounts. Territory & Partner Development • Demonstrate strong knowledge of the Southern Brazilian business environment, including regional industries, enterprise accounts, and key economic groups. • Establish and strengthen relationships with local technology partners, system integrators, and channel organizations. • Develop joint go-to-market initiatives with regional partners to generate qualified pipeline and accelerate revenue. • Identify high-potential accounts and market segments, creating a sustainable territory development strategy. Sales Execution & Forecasting • Consistently achieve or exceed sales quotas for new business and account expansion. • Maintain a healthy, qualified, and predictable sales pipeline. • Provide accurate forecasts and maintain disciplined CRM hygiene. • Apply consultative and value-based selling methodologies to articulate business outcomes and return on investment. • Collaborate with Sales Engineering, Marketing, Channel Sales, Customer Success, and leadership to execute strategic opportunities.
