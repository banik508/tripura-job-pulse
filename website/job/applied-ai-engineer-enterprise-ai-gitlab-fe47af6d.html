An overview of this role As an Applied AI Engineer at GitLab, you'll build and deploy AI-powered solutions for GitLab's own teams. You'll join Enterprise AI in the CIO organization, reporting to the Senior Director, Enterprise AI. This is a hands-on engineering role: you'll write production code and partner with senior engineers and internal business stakeholders to solve problems and deliver measurable outcomes. Your focus is GitLab's internal operations, rather than customer implementations or development of the GitLab product. Building fast matters, and we want engineers who ship. But speed without understanding the problem leads to the wrong solution shipped quickly. You'll partner with senior engineers and stakeholders to understand the real problem first: how work moves across teams, where the real constraint lives, and whether AI is the right intervention. From there, you'll take ownership of the build and deployment, combining strong engineering skills with curiosity about the business systems you're improving. Your initial focus will span GitLab's own Sales, Marketing, and Customer Support teams, where you'll embed AI solutions into the systems and workflows they use every day. This role offers the opportunity to grow as an engineer while shaping how GitLab team members work, in a remote, asynchronous, and values-driven environment. What you'll do • Contribute to problem diagnosis alongside the team. Engage with stakeholders to understand the real constraint before reaching for a solution, and be willing to flag when AI isn't the right answer. • Own the build and deployment of AI solutions, from technical design through implementation, deployment, and iteration. Partner with senior engineers on discovery and scoping. • Design, develop, and ship AI-powered solutions quickly, delivering working prototypes in days, not months, with a focus on practical outcomes and measurable business value. • Build solutions that reduce bottlenecks, shorten lead times, and increase throughput. Track flow, adoption, and ROI metrics defined for the work. • Integrate AI capabilities into GitLab's internal business systems and workflows using APIs, orchestration tools, and modern AI platforms, including GitLab Duo Agent Platform, where appropriate. The right tool wins, whether that's custom code, a platform, or a well-crafted prompt. • Be Customer Zero: use GitLab's AI products in our own internal workflows, assess how they perform in practice, and share feedback with R&D to help improve them.…
