About the job
Job Description About the Role: Scale the advertisement platform at Swiggy by working closely with Sales, Business and Product teams, with the objective of making ad sales a key contributor to Swiggy's revenue. Key Responsibilities: • Coordinate with Sales teams and City Heads to meet monthly, quarterly and annual revenue targets (MOP, QOP and AOP). • Share opportunity areas with city leadership and close deals. • Get commitments and deals from partners in association with the Sales team. • Ensure ROI delivery for all performance-linked selling. • Manage campaign performance through daily campaign optimisation and drive GOV growth for brand partners. • Ensure retention and upsell on ads. • Improve ads depth across classifiers and reach out to partners to increase width. • Find new opportunities on ads and new requirements in the market. • Launch new ads programs and drive their go-to-market. • Coordinate with Sales teams across all cities to ensure timely and consistent campaign execution. • Define go-to-market strategies specific to each geography to unlock new sources of revenue. • Coordinate with Finance and Controllership teams. • Liaise with Product and Merchandising teams to define SOPs and oversee execution. Qualifications Qualifications and Skills: • Graduation from IIT / NIT. • 2–3 years of experience. • MBA from a Tier-1 college (optional). • Very strong analytics and communication skills, with experience in sales coordination. • Proven expertise in stakeholder management, with a track record of meeting and exceeding targets. • Go-getter attitude: Strong self-motivation and resilience in pursuing opportunities. • Accountability: Ability to own end-to-end conversations with stakeholders and deliver high- quality results in a fast-paced environment. • Team leadership: Strong capability to inspire and guide a team toward success. Additional Information Visit our tech blogs to learn more about some the challenges we deal with: https://medium.com/swiggy-bytes We are an equal opportunity employer and all qualified applicants will receive consideration for employment without regard to race, colour, religion, sex, disability status, or any other characteristic protected by the law. Company Description About Swiggy: Swiggy is India’s leading on-demand delivery platform with a tech-first approach to logistics and a solution-first approach to consumer demands. With a presence in 500+ cities across India, partnerships with hundreds of thousands of restaurants, an employee base of over 5000, a 2 lakh+ strong independent fleet of Delivery Executives, we deliver unparalleled convenience driven by continuous innovation. Built on the back of robust ML technology and fueled by terabytes of data processed every day, Swiggy offers a fast, seamless and reliable delivery experience for millions of customers across India. From starting out as a hyperlocal food delivery service in 2014, to becoming India’s leading on- demand convenience platform today, our capabilities result not only in lightning-fast delivery for customers, but also in a productive and fulfilling experience for our employees.
