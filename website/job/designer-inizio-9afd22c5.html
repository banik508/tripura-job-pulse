About the job
We invite you to come grow with us. Inizio Ignite is the global advisory partner for health and life sciences, bringing together connected expertise across Research Partnership, Putnam, Vynamic and STEM. Guided by purpose, passion and precision, we connect insight, strategy and execution to help clients make stronger decisions, accelerate performance and deliver lasting impact for patients. Data, technology and AI enhance this work, alongside the human expertise and judgement that define our advisory offer. About the India Design Studio We are establishing an India Design Studio in Gurugram to support Inizio Ignite's global teams. The studio will operate as a central design hub, working with business-unit spokes across Research Partnership, Putnam, Vynamic, STEM and Innovation & Growth. The Design Studio will turn complex strategic, scientific and commercial content into clear, persuasive client stories. It will support high-value business development and client-facing work, including proposals, RFP responses, pitch decks, credentials, case studies, thought leadership and other priority materials. Our goal is to improve the quality, speed and consistency of these materials; strengthen how Ignite presents its connected expertise; and build reusable design assets and ways of working that support client delivery and growth. How quality works in the studio Quality is shared. Everyone who creates or edits work is accountable for the accuracy and quality of their contribution. Designers complete a structured self-check before handover. The Production & QA Assistant provides independent, checklist-based review and delivery control. The Senior Designer provides creative oversight and final design sign-off for priority or high-risk work. Business stakeholders remain accountable for factual content, client requirements and final business approval. How we use AI Approved AI and automation tools may be used to support brief understanding, content structuring, asset discovery, layout exploration, tagging and quality checks. They are enabling tools rather than substitutes for design craft. Human judgement, confidentiality, accessibility, brand integrity and final accountability always come first. Your primary role Designer - India Design Studio This role is a developing presentation designer who delivers high-quality, client-facing work within agreed standards and timelines. You will improve the clarity, structure and visual impact of proposals, RFP responses, pitch decks and related materials, while building the judgement and independence required to progress in the team. What you will do • Design and redesign slides, proposal sections, RFP materials, credentials and case studies to improve clarity, visual hierarchy, structure and readability. • Apply Inizio Ignite brand standards, PowerPoint templates, visual systems and document standards consistently. • Translate clear briefs and developed content into polished visual outputs, escalating gaps or ambiguity early. • Adapt existing materials for different audiences, clients, geographies and pursuit contexts. • Create reusable slides, diagrams, icons and data visuals. • Work with the design team and the Senior Designer on more complex visual storytelling and priority pursuits. • Own the quality of your work, complete the studio self-check and resolve issues identified through design review or independent QA. • Maintain clean, editable, accessible, well-named and version-controlled files that are ready for handover and reuse. • Use approved AI tools responsibly where they improve understanding, exploration or efficiency, while applying independent design judgement to every output. • Contribute to asset tagging, template improvement, knowledge capture and shared ways of working.
