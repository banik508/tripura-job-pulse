About the job
Job Description Key Responsibilities • Enterprise Account Management: Own a portfolio of strategic enterprise accounts across MEA, building strong relationships with key stakeholders throughout the customer lifecycle. • Customer Retention: Drive retention and renewal outcomes, ensuring high customer satisfaction and achievement of GRR targets. • Strategic Engagement: Build relationships with executive sponsors, decision-makers, and key stakeholders to understand business objectives and align Freshworks solutions to their needs. • Risk & Churn Management: Proactively identify at-risk accounts, develop recovery plans, and drive interventions to mitigate churn. • Value Realization & Adoption: Drive product adoption and ensure customers maximize value from Freshworks solutions. • Revenue Growth: Partner with Account Managers and Sales teams to identify expansion opportunities across the assigned portfolio. • Cross-functional Collaboration: Work with Support, Product, Billing, and internal teams to resolve escalations and address customer concerns. • Account Planning: Own account health, renewal forecasting, retention plans, and strategic engagement across the portfolio. Qualifications • 6–10 years of experience in Customer Success or Strategic Account Management within a SaaS/enterprise technology environment. • Proven experience managing strategic enterprise accounts and driving customer retention and renewals. • Strong understanding of GRR, churn management, account health, and customer lifecycle management. • Experience engaging senior stakeholders and executive decision-makers. • Strong commercial acumen, communication, negotiation, and stakeholder management skills. • Ability to independently manage complex enterprise relationships and drive outcomes across the MEA region. • Prior experience managing MEA customers would be an added advantage.
