About the job
Job Description • Lead, mentor, and develop a team of Account Executives, establishing clear performance standards and supporting continuous professional development and career advancement. • Partner directly with Account Executives on key strategic accounts to structure deals, navigate multi-stakeholder evaluations, and lead negotiations with senior executives. • Oversee day-to-day sales operations, enforcing pipeline generation discipline, sales methodology rigor, and accurate weekly forecasting. • Drive vertical go-to-market execution by identifying sector-specific trends, customer pain points, and use cases to position Freshworks' product suite effectively. • Collaborate cross-functionally with Solution Engineering, Field Marketing, Customer Success, Channel, and Business Development to accelerate deal velocity and ensure cohesive account engagement. • Anticipate execution risks within deal cycles and proactively implement mitigation strategies to maintain momentum and deal velocity. • Establish team practices that encourage adaptability, operational rigor, and systematic sharing of deal wins and competitive insights. Qualifications • 10–15 years of software sales experience, including 3-5 years of direct people management experience leading quota-carrying B2B SaaS sales teams. • Proven track record of consistently meeting or exceeding revenue targets through value-based and solution-selling methodologies in mid-market or enterprise segments. • Strong skills around executing Mutual Action Plans, deal qualification, and complex deal negotiations. • Ability to coach others while managing to expectations, • Demonstrated commitment to investing in people by building high-trust, inclusive team environments, coaching sellers through complex cycles, and developing emerging talent. • Customer-centric orientation with the ability to translate industry-specific operational needs into scalable software solutions and engage credibly with C-level stakeholders. • Strong execution focus with hands-on experience utilizing CRM reporting, pipeline metrics, and sales forecasting cadences to drive team performance. • Effective cross-functional collaborator experienced in aligning sales motions with marketing campaigns, partner networks, and product specialists. • Must be highly motivated, carry an entrepreneurial spirit for the team, and display an incredibly high sense of urgency and execution. Nice to Have • Domain expertise selling into the Manufacturing vertical. • Previous experience selling Customer Experience (CX), CRM, or IT Service Management (ITSM) enterprise software platforms. Additional Information The annual base salary range for this position is $195,000 - $240,000 + Variable Compensation (where applicable) Compensation is based on a variety of factors including but not limited to location, experience, job-related skills, and level. Bonus/equity may be available. Freshworks Benefits: Freshworks offers multiple options for dental, medical, vision, disability, and life insurance. Equity + ESPP, flexible PTO, flexible spending, commuter benefits and wellness benefits are also offered. Freshworks also offers adoption and parental leave benefits.
