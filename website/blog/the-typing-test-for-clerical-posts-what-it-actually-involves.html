The typing test for clerical posts: what it actually involves

Thirty words a minute in English, twenty-five in Bengali, on a computer keyboard, and it carries no marks. What the rules say, and why people still fail it.

LDCtyping testclericalpreparation

Almost everything written about typing tests online is about the SSC exam. Very little of it describes what a Lower Division Clerk candidate in Tripura actually faces, and the difference matters.

Here is what the recruitment rules say — in this case the revised rules for the LDC post under the Rural Development Department, dated 2024.

The numbers

English typing: a minimum accurate speed of 30 words per minute.

Bengali typing: a minimum of 25 words per minute, for selection as a Bengali Typist.

Both on a computer with a keyboard — the rules say so explicitly. The old manual typewriter is gone. If you have been practising on a typewriter because that is what the coaching centre has, you are preparing for the wrong machine.

Alongside the speed, the rules require basic computer knowledge and "knowledge of operating Computer and proficiency in typing on computer with Keyboard".

It carries no marks

This is the part most people do not know, and it changes how you should think about it.

The type test is qualifying only. It carries no marks and does not add to your position in the merit list. Typing at 60 words a minute does not move you up. Typing at 29 does not move you down — it simply means you have not qualified yet.

The selection itself is 80% by direct recruitment and 20% by promotion. For direct recruitment it is a written examination worth 85% of the marks and an interview worth 15%, and the type test comes after that. The written paper covers English or Bengali, job-oriented questions, and general knowledge and current affairs.

So the order of your effort should be: written paper first, by a long way. Then make sure the typing cannot trip you at the end.

Your rank is decided by the written exam. The typing test only decides whether that rank counts.

There is a second chance built in

Something else worth knowing. Under these rules, a candidate who does not pass the type test initially can still be recruited, on consolidated pay for six months, and is then retested.

That is not a reason to arrive unprepared. Six months on consolidated pay is six months of worry, and the retest is real. But it is worth knowing that one bad morning is not automatically the end of it.

There is also an exemption: a person with a disability certified by a State Medical Board or a registered government medical officer as unable to type is exempt from both the typing qualification and the test.

Why people fail a test they could pass

Nearly always one of four things.

They practise speed and ignore accuracy. The rules say accurate speed. Errors are deducted. Someone typing 45 words a minute with ten mistakes often scores below someone typing 32 cleanly. Slow down until you are accurate, then build speed on top of accuracy — never the other way round.

They practise on their phone. Thumbs on glass is a different skill from fingers on a keyboard. It builds nothing you can use.

They have never used that keyboard layout. Bengali typing in particular depends on the layout installed on the machine. Find out which layout the test uses and practise on that one, not a different one you happen to have.

They have never typed under a clock. Typing at home with the radio on is not the same as typing in a hall with an invigilator and a timer. The first time you feel that pressure should not be on the day.

How to practise

Ten focused minutes a day beats an hour once a week, because typing is muscle memory and muscle memory is built by repetition, not duration.

  • Sit properly, both hands on the keyboard, and stop looking down.
  • Type full passages, not single words. The test is a passage.
  • Count your errors every time, not just your speed.
  • Do it at the same time each day until it is automatic.

Our typing test is timed and shows you both speed and accuracy, so you can watch the gap between them close. Practise until your accurate speed is comfortably above the requirement — aim for 40 in English, not 30, so that nerves on the day still leave you above the line.

One important caution

The figures above come from one department's recruitment rules. Each recruitment publishes its own rules, and they differ. Some posts ask for a different speed, some have a different weighting between the written exam and the interview, some do not have a typing test at all.

Always read the notification for the post you are applying to. If it says something different from this article, the notification is right and this article is not.

Thirty words a minute is not a high bar. It is an achievable one in a few weeks of daily practice, and it is a strange thing to lose a post over after clearing the written paper.
