About the job
Job Description As a Senior Staff Product Manager in the ITSM Product team, you will play a pivotal role in shaping the next generation of enterprise service management. This is an opportunity to challenge conventional thinking, identify disruptive opportunities and redefine how enterprises manage IT services and operations. You will operate at the intersection of ITSM, ITAM, ITOM and Artificial Intelligence, driving the evolution from traditional ticket-centric service management to intelligent, proactive and autonomous enterprise operations. We are looking for a product leader who combines deep domain expertise with a strong innovation mindset, someone who can identify emerging customer problems, envision transformative solutions and translate them into differentiated products with significant business impact. You will work closely with Engineering, AI, Design, GTM and Customer Success teams to take ideas from concept to market, influence strategic product investments and drive meaningful outcomes for customers and the business. This is a high-impact individual contributor role that requires the ability to think strategically, operate independently and influence across organizational boundaries. Impact You Will Create Reimagine the Future of ITSM Challenge traditional ITSM paradigms and define the next generation of enterprise service management experiences. Identify opportunities to move beyond reactive ticket management towards proactive, intelligent and autonomous IT operations. Lead 0-to-1 initiatives that have the potential to fundamentally change how enterprises operate. Shape AI-Native Product Experiences Leverage Generative AI, Agentic AI and intelligent automation to rethink existing workflows and create new ways of delivering enterprise services. Explore opportunities for autonomous resolution, contextual intelligence and intelligent orchestration. Influence Product Strategy & Business Outcomes Translate market opportunities and customer insights into compelling product strategies and roadmaps. Drive initiatives that strengthen competitive differentiation, accelerate adoption and create meaningful business impact. Champion Customer-Centric Innovation Engage deeply with enterprise customers to understand their operational challenges, validate new concepts and identify unmet needs. Connect customer problems with emerging technology capabilities to develop scalable solutions. Elevate Product Thinking Bring strong product judgment, strategic thinking and systems-level problem solving to complex product challenges. Influence and inspire cross-functional teams to raise the bar on product innovation and execution.
