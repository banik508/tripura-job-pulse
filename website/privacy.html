Who we are

Tripura Job Pulse is an independent job listing and exam preparation website for Tripura, at tripurajobpulse.com. It is not a government website and is not affiliated with TPSC, JRBT, TRBT, any government department or any employer.

For anything about your data, write to tripurajobpulse@gmail.com.

What we collect

If you only browse

You can read job listings, current affairs and the home page without an account. We count visits so we know which pages are useful. Those counts do not carry your name, your email address or your IP address, and we cannot work out who you are from them.

If you create an account

WhatWhere it comes from
Email addressYou, at sign-up
Full name, mobile number, district, date of birthYou, on your profile page
Education, work experience, skills, languagesYou, on your profile page
Mock test attempts and scoresCreated when you take a test
Typing test attempts, speed and accuracyCreated when you take a typing test
Skill certificates you earnCreated when you pass an exam
Payment recordsCreated when you buy a plan or a study module by UPI

Your profile is optional. The site works without it; the CV builder does not, because it builds your CV from exactly those fields.

If you ask for job alerts

Your email address and, if you choose one, your district. Nothing else.

If you write to us or post a vacancy

Your name, phone number, email address and your message. If you post a vacancy as an employer, we also keep the vacancy details and the contact person you give, so that we can ring you to verify it before publishing.

Why we collect it

  • To run your account — signing you in, remembering your plan
  • To build your CV — the CV builder fills itself from your profile
  • To mark your tests and issue a certificate that an employer can verify
  • To send the job alerts you asked for, and nothing else by email
  • To verify a vacancy before it goes on the site
  • To see which pages are used, so we know what to improve

We do not use your data to profile you for advertising.

Who else sees it

We do not sell your personal data, and we never will. We do not pass your profile, your phone number or your email address to employers or to anybody else for marketing.

We will also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and profile — until you delete it
  • Job alert subscriptions — until you unsubscribe
  • Messages and vacancy submissions — until they are dealt with, then periodically cleared
  • Visit counts — kept as statistics, with nothing in them that identifies you

Your choices

Delete your account at any time, yourself. Go to Delete your account, press the button and confirm. It removes your login, profile, test attempts, certificates and payment records straight away. There is no fee and you do not have to give a reason.

  • See what we hold — your profile page shows everything you have given us
  • Correct it — edit your profile at any time
  • Stop the emails — the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any job alert
  • Ask us to delete it for you — if you cannot sign in, use the form on the deletion page or email us

Children

This site is for people looking for work and preparing for recruitment examinations. It is not intended for children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has given us information, write to us and we will remove it.

Keeping it safe

The site is served over HTTPS. Your password is stored by Supabase in hashed form, so nobody here can read it. Access to the database is restricted to the site administrator.

We will never ask you for your UPI PIN, a password or an OTP. Nobody from Tripura Job Pulse will ever ask for these. If someone does, it is not us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. If the change is significant and you have an account, we will tell you by email.

Contact

Questions, corrections or complaints: tripurajobpulse@gmail.com. We reply to every message about personal data within seven days.
