Declaration and important notice — please read before applying
  • Tripura Job Pulse is a private information platform. It is not a government website and is not affiliated with TPSC, JRBT, TRBT, any government department or any employer.
  • We never charge any fee to apply for a job, and we never collect application fees on behalf of an employer. Always apply through the official link or notification.
  • Job details are compiled from public notifications and other sources. Before applying, always check the official notification, which is the final authority on eligibility, dates and fees.
  • Our certificates confirm skills assessed in our own online exams. They are not university degrees or government qualifications, and are not affiliated with or endorsed by Microsoft or any other software maker.
  • Study material and current affairs are for exam preparation. We work hard on accuracy, but errors are possible, so verify anything critical with official sources.
  • Payments are made directly by UPI and activated after we confirm them. We never ask for your UPI PIN, passwords or OTP. Nobody from Tripura Job Pulse will ever ask for these.
  • We do not sell your personal data. Your profile is used to run your account and to build your CV.
