About the job
About the Team Meesho is the most downloaded e-commerce app in India. As part of the core Business and Product Experience team, we are taking a massive leap from a purely transactional e-commerce platform to a destination for visual discovery and personal inspiration. We are building an immersive feed inside the Meesho app, focused on look-based, trend-based, and highly personalized inspiration across major lifestyle categories like Fashion, Home, and Beauty. The DAU Labs team focuses on building engaging discovery experiences, scaling content supply, and solving complex personalization and ranking problems to drive user engagement, daily active users (DAU), and long-term retention. About the Role As a Product Manager II at DAU Labs, you will play a key role in building and scaling Meesho's content-led discovery experience. You will work on creating engaging and personalized product experiences that help millions of users discover relevant trends, products, and inspiration. You will own key product problems across content discovery, personalization, user engagement, and conversion. Working closely with Engineering, Data Science, Design, and Business teams, you will identify opportunities, drive product improvements, and deliver measurable impact on DAU growth and user retention. What you will do • Product Strategy & Ownership: Own key product areas within the content discovery ecosystem, identify user and business problems, and define product roadmaps aligned with DAU growth objectives. • Discovery & Personalization: Work on improving content discovery, feed relevance, recommendation systems, and personalization to deliver engaging experiences tailored to different user cohorts. • User Engagement & Growth: Identify opportunities to improve user engagement, daily active usage, session frequency, and long-term retention through data-driven product initiatives. • Experimentation & Analytics: Drive product experiments, A/B testing, and data analysis to identify growth opportunities, measure product performance, and continuously improve user experience. • Product Execution: Work closely with Engineering, Design, and Data Science teams to translate business problems into product requirements and drive end-to-end execution. • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner with Content, Category Marketing, Seller Experience, and other business teams to improve content quality, discovery, and content-to-commerce conversion. • 0-to-1 Problem Solving: Identify new opportunities, solve ambiguous product problems, and build scalable product solutions that create meaningful user and business impact What you will need • Experience: Overall experience of 4+ years, with at least 4+ years of experience in Product Management in a tech-led company. Experience in B2C or consumer internet companies is preferred. • Product & Domain Expertise: Experience working on consumer-facing products, content-led platforms, discovery experiences, recommendation systems, personalization, or engagement-focused products is preferred. • Analytical & Problem-Solving Skills: Strong analytical aptitude with the ability to solve complex and ambiguous problems related to user discovery, engagement, personalization, and product growth. • Execution Bias: Strong bias for action and execution, with the ability to manage multiple priorities and deliver outcomes in a fast-paced environment. • Data-Driven Mindset: Ability to use data, user insights, and experimentation to identify opportunities, make product decisions, and measure outcomes. • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Ability to work effectively with Engineering, Design, Data Science, and Business teams to drive product initiatives end to end. • Mindset: Strong passion for Problem First Solving (PFS), ownership, and building scalable products from 0 to 1.
