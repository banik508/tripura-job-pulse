About the job
Why Mozilla? Mozilla Corporation is the non-profit-backed technology company that has shaped the internet for the better over the last 25 years. We make pioneering brands like Firefox, the privacy-minded web browser. Now, with more than 225 million people around the world using our products each month, we’re shaping the next 25 years of technology and helping to reclaim an internet built for people, not companies. Our work focuses on diverse areas including AI, social media, security and more. And we’re doing this while never losing our focus on our core mission – to make the internet better for people. The Mozilla Corporation is wholly owned by the non-profit 501(c) Mozilla Foundation. This means we aren’t beholden to any shareholders — only to our mission. Along with thousands of volunteer contributors and collaborators all over the world, Mozillians design, build and distribute open-source software that enables people to enjoy the internet on their terms. About this team and role: Mozilla is seeking a Staff Compensation Analyst with expert-level Excel skills and deep expertise in data analysis and analytics to help develop and deliver compensation programs for our global workforce. You will work with complex datasets, build analytical models, and turn findings into clear recommendations that inform compensation decisions. As a member of a small compensation team, you will independently lead assigned programs and projects while providing support and backup across other areas. Your work will span market pricing, salary structures, annual compensation planning, executive compensation, sales incentives, and consultation with People Business Partners and Talent Acquisition. This is an individual contributor role requiring strong analytical judgment, substantial program ownership, and the ability to improve how we use data and deliver our work. What you’ll do: • Lead compensation analysis and modeling. Combine employee, market, and financial data to assess competitiveness, internal equity, pay distribution, and program effectiveness. Build models that help leaders evaluate options, costs, and employee impact. • Build and maintain advanced Excel tools. Develop accurate, scalable models and reporting tools using advanced formulas, lookup functions, pivot tables, and data validation. Structure work so that calculations are traceable, assumptions are clear, and outputs are easy to maintain. • Strengthen data quality and reporting. Extract, reconcile, and analyze data from Workday and other sources. Investigate discrepancies, establish validation checks, and improve the consistency and reliability of compensation reporting. • Own compensation programs and projects. Manage assigned work from planning through implementation, including analysis, recommendations, stakeholder coordination, communications, and ongoing administration. • Lead market analysis and job pricing. Evaluate roles, match jobs to market surveys, assess competitive positioning, and recommend compensation ranges. Apply a consistent approach to job levels and internal comparisons across functions and countries. • Maintain salary structures. Analyze market movement and employee positioning, model potential changes, and recommend updates that balance competitiveness, internal equity, and cost. • Deliver annual compensation planning. Develop planning tools and guidelines, validate employee data, model budgets and outcomes, support decision makers, and coordinate accurate implementation with People Operations and Finance. • Support executive compensation. Conduct benchmarking, model compensation scenarios, prepare materials for leadership and Compensation Committee review, and administer assigned executive compensation processes. • Support sales compensation. Partner with business leaders, Sales Operations, and Finance on incentive plan design, modeling, documentation, and administration. Help resolve questions about plan application and payout calculations.…
