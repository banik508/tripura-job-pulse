About the job
Job Description We are seeking a detail-oriented and collaborative Assistant Manager - HRBP to join our Human Resources team in Bengaluru, India. In this role, you will serve as a strategic HR Business Partner, supporting our organization's workforce by bridging HR initiatives with business objectives. You will work closely with department managers and employees to ensure effective HR service delivery, policy implementation, and employee engagement while maintaining a professional and supportive approach to all HR functions. • Partner with business leaders and department managers to understand organizational needs and develop HR strategies that align with business goals • Support the full employee lifecycle, including recruitment, onboarding, performance management, and offboarding processes • Manage employee relations matters, including conflict resolution, disciplinary actions, and grievance handling while maintaining confidentiality and professionalism • Assist in the development and implementation of HR policies, procedures, and programs that promote a positive workplace culture • Analyze HR data and metrics to identify trends, generate reports, and provide actionable insights to senior HR leadership • Coordinate training and development initiatives to support employee skill enhancement and career growth opportunities • Ensure compliance with labor laws, regulations, and organizational policies across all HR functions • Maintain and update HRIS systems and HR documentation with accuracy and timeliness • Support recruitment efforts by coordinating job postings, screening candidates, and facilitating the interview process • Serve as a point of contact for employee inquiries regarding HR policies, benefits, compensation, and career development • Participate in HR projects and special initiatives as assigned by senior management Qualifications **Required Qualifications:** • 3-5 years of experience in HR operations, employee relations, or HR business partnership roles • Strong knowledge of HR processes, employment laws, and organizational policies • Proficiency with HRIS systems and HR software platforms • Excellent written and verbal communication skills with the ability to interact professionally with all organizational levels • Strong organizational and time management abilities with attention to detail • Demonstrated problem-solving skills and ability to handle sensitive HR matters with discretion • Proficiency in data analysis and HR reporting tools • Ability to work collaboratively with cross-functional teams and manage multiple priorities simultaneously **Preferred Qualifications:** • Experience in HR analytics and workforce planning • Knowledge of compensation and benefits administration • Familiarity with training and development program coordination • Experience with change management initiatives • Project management experience within an HR context • Exposure to recruitment and talent acquisition processes • HR certification or professional development in human resources **Domain Expertise:** • Human
