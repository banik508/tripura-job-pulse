About the job
Why Mozilla? Mozilla Corporation is the non-profit-backed technology company that has shaped the internet for the better over the last 25 years. We make pioneering brands like Firefox, the privacy-minded web browser. Now, with more than 225 million people around the world using our products each month, we’re shaping the next 25 years of technology and helping to reclaim an internet built for people, not companies. Our work focuses on diverse areas including AI, social media, security and more. And we’re doing this while never losing our focus on our core mission – to make the internet better for people. The Mozilla Corporation is wholly owned by the non-profit 501(c) Mozilla Foundation. This means we aren’t beholden to any shareholders — only to our mission. Along with thousands of volunteer contributors and collaborators all over the world, Mozillians design, build and distribute open-source software that enables people to enjoy the internet on their terms. About this team and role: Strategic Finance is a high-visibility, high-impact role embedded in the company’s decision-making core. You will partner with our executive, product, and go-to-market (GTM) teams, owning the financial modeling and analysis that drives our product and corporate strategy, optimizes our GTM engine, and informs critical operational decisions. What you’ll do: • Partner with Product and GTM leaders to evaluate the financial ROI and viability of new products, business lines, and R&D investments. • Own the long-range plan, connecting product and revenue metrics to financial outcomes and keeping reporting accurate, timely, and consistent. • Build and maintain financial models that translate operating plans into outcomes, risks, and opportunities, including unit economics, pricing analysis, and scenario planning. • Lead cross-functional initiatives on resource allocation, operational efficiency, and scaling business processes. What you’ll bring: • 5+ years of experience in strategic finance, FP&A, corporate finance, or investment banking. • Bachelor’s degree in finance, accounting, economics, or a related field, or equivalent experience. • Strong financial modeling skills, with a rigorous, quantitative approach to forecasting, budgeting, and measuring operational performance. • Exceptional communication skills, with the ability to turn complex financial concepts into clear narratives for non-financial stakeholders and to partner with senior leaders. • A high standard for operational excellence and a clear sense of how to turn ambiguous business questions into actionable plans. • Experience using AI tools to work faster and better, and a willingness to introduce them to your team and cross-functional partners. • Commitment to our values: • Welcoming differences • Being relationship-minded • Practicing responsible participation • Having grit Nice to haves: • Experience with software, internet, consumption-based, subscription, advertising, or AI-driven business models. • Experience with ERP and planning tools such as NetSuite, Workday, Pigment, or Adaptive.
