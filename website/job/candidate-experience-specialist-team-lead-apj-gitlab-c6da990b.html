An overview of this role As the Candidate Experience Team Lead for [Region], you'll own how candidates experience GitLab across your region by raising the capability of the specialists around you. You'll set the quality bar, balance workloads, onboard and develop specialists, and serve as the first escalation point when a process breaks down or a candidate situation needs judgment. You'll stay hands-on with interview coordination, background checks, and offers, while spending most of your time making the regional function run well. You'll use candidate experience data to surface patterns and risks to the Director, Talent Acquisition and Enablement, and partner with leads in other regions to maintain one global process. At GitLab, our mission is simple: to enable everyone to contribute to and co-create the software that powers our world. What you’ll do • Lead Candidate Experience Specialists in [Region], setting and modeling standards for interview coordination, candidate communication, contract and offer accuracy, and data hygiene in the applicant tracking system. • Balance coordination work across the regional team, monitor capacity against hiring volume, and flag resourcing gaps to the Director, Talent Acquisition and Enablement before they affect candidates. • Resolve escalated scheduling conflicts, candidate issues, and process gaps, bringing clear recommendations to leadership when further support is needed. • Own onboarding, ramp, and ongoing development for new specialists through documentation, shadowing, and direct feedback on work quality. • Coordinate interviews, prepare offers and employment contracts, manage background and reference checks, and partner with People Operations on the handoff to onboarding. • Build and maintain reporting on candidate satisfaction, specialist response times, scheduling turnaround, and data accuracy to identify patterns, bottlenecks, and risks. • Partner with leads in other regions to maintain one global coordination process, contribute regional evidence to process and tooling improvements, implement agreed changes, and keep documentation accurate in the applicant tracking system and GitLab repositories. • Collaborate with recruiters, hiring managers, Talent Acquisition leadership, Information Technology, and People Operations across time zones to keep hiring moving, modeling GitLab's values, culture, and all-remote approach in every interaction.
