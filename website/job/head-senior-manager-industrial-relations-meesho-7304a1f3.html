About the job
About the Team You will join the Security & Investigations team within Fulfilment & Experience (F&E) at Meesho. F&E is the operational and technological backbone powering India's cost-efficient, reliable and scalable e-commerce network, serving 100M+ monthly active users. From enabling Valmo's distributed logistics network to building scalable fulfilment and experience platforms, F&E operates at the intersection of technology, operations and network innovation. Within F&E, the Security & Investigations team safeguards the integrity and resilience of this network. The team investigates shipment loss, pilferage, fraud and misconduct, oversees physical security across operating sites, and coordinates with law enforcement and other stakeholders where required. Working closely with Operations, HR, Legal and logistics partners, the team helps keep the network secure, compliant and resilient. About the Role Valmo's operating network is powered by a large and diverse workforce spanning sort centres, hubs and last-mile operations. A significant portion of this workforce is engaged through logistics partners and other workforce arrangements, creating a complex industrial-relations environment across multiple locations and states. As Head – Industrial Relations, you will own the Industrial Relations (IR) charter for the operating network, within the Security & Investigations function, and work closely with HR, Legal, Operations and partner leadership. Your mandate will be to build a stable, compliant and scalable workforce-relations framework that prevents avoidable disputes, enables fair and timely resolution of workforce issues, and provides strong governance when matters escalate. The role combines strategic IR leadership with significant field engagement. You will build early-warning mechanisms, manage worker and union relationships, oversee dispute and disciplinary processes, strengthen partner workforce compliance, engage with labour authorities, and create playbooks and governance mechanisms that can scale with the network. What You Will Do Own IR strategy and governance. Build and continuously evolve a national IR framework covering workforce relations, grievance management, dispute prevention, statutory compliance, worker/union engagement, escalation and resolution. Build workforce intelligence and early-warning mechanisms. Track workforce sentiment, grievances, absenteeism, wage-related concerns, shift and working-condition issues, emerging worker representation or union activity, and other indicators of potential disruption across the network. Prevent and manage industrial disputes. Develop structured approaches to prevent disputes and lead the response to strikes, work stoppages, protests, gheraos and other workforce disruptions. Engage with worker representatives and union leaders and coordinate with Operations, HR, Legal, partners, local administration, labour authorities and law enforcement, as appropriate. Bring IR rigour to investigations. Partner with Security & Investigations, HR and Legal on cases involving employees or partner workers, including theft, fraud, misconduct and policy violations. Ensure due process, appropriate documentation and legally sound decision-making. Govern disciplinary processes. Oversee show-cause notices, domestic enquiries and disciplinary actions, ensuring that processes are fair, consistent, timely and legally defensible under applicable law, standing orders and organisational policy. Drive partner workforce compliance. Establish and monitor governance over logistics partners covering contract labour requirements, wage and benefits compliance, statutory registrations, working hours, worker documentation, grievance mechanisms and other applicable labour requirements. Drive timely corrective action and escalation where gaps are identified.…
